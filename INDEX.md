# INDEX — the brain's catalog

Skim this to find the right note, then open only what the task needs. Keep it current:
**every new note gets one line here** (one exception: `ideas/` — one line per
category, not per idea).

> The entries below describe the starter examples. Replace them as your real notes
> take their place — the examples exist to show the format, not to be kept.
